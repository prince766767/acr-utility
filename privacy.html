Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What the app is

ACR Utility helps a teacher fill in their own Annual Confidential Report (UGC PBAS / ACR form) and make the Word file. It is a web page with no server of its own: everything runs in your browser.

What is stored, and where

  • What you type is saved only in your own browser on your device, so you can continue later. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • You can export a copy of your draft as a file and delete it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.
  • The app collects no analytics, uses no advertising and sets no tracking cookies.

Google Drive and sharing (only when you use them)

  • Save to Google Drive and Share / Email ask you to sign in with your Google account. The app asks only for the permission "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file). It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your Word file and PDF are saved in your own Google Drive, in the folder "ACR Utility - Word and PDF". To make the PDF, the Word file is briefly converted by Google Docs in your Drive and that temporary copy is deleted straight away.
  • Share / Email hands the files to the share menu of your own device (for example Gmail); you choose where they go.
  • The sign-in permission is kept only while the page is open and is never stored. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
