Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

What the app is

ACR Utility helps a teacher fill in their own Annual Confidential Report (UGC PBAS / ACR form) and make the Word file. It is a web page with no server of its own: everything runs in your browser.

What is stored, and where

  • What you type is saved only in your own browser on your device, so you can continue later. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • You can export a copy of your draft as a file and delete it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.
  • The app collects no analytics, uses no advertising and sets no tracking cookies.
  • Rating and feedback (Review tab) are sent only if you choose to: the app opens your own email app with your rating and feedback text, and you press Send. The email goes to the developer and shows your email address, as any email does. Nothing from your ACR is added to it.

Google Drive and sharing (only when you use them)

  • Save to Google Drive and Share / Email ask you to sign in with your Google account. The app asks only for the permission "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file). It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your Word file and PDF are saved in your own Google Drive, in the folder "ACR Utility - Word and PDF". To make the PDF, the Word file is briefly converted by Google Docs in your Drive and that temporary copy is deleted straight away.
  • If you switch on Keep my draft in Google Drive, your draft is also saved as a file in that same folder of your own Drive, so you can continue on another device signed in with the same Google account.
  • Share / Email hands the files to the share menu of your own device (for example Gmail); you choose where they go.
  • To record that a Google account accepted this policy, the app reads that account's email address from Google when you sign in. The address and the date are kept only in your own browser on that device; they are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The sign-in permission is kept only while the page is open and is never stored. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Accepting this policy

  • The first time you open the app on a device, you are asked to accept this policy before you can use the form.
  • Every Google account (email) is also asked to accept it once on each device, straight after it signs in. If you choose "Not now", the sign-in is dropped and only the Google features stay off.

Your responsibility for your data

  • Your entries are kept in your own browser and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. They can be lost, for example if the browser's site data is cleared, the device is changed, lost or damaged, a file is deleted from your Drive, or because of a fault in the app, the browser or a Google service.
  • You are responsible for your own data and for keeping a backup (use Export Draft, and keep the Word and PDF files you make). The app is provided free of charge, as it is, without any warranty. The developer is not responsible for any loss of data, however it happens.
  • Please check the generated Word / PDF file yourself before you submit it.
