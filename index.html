        Make a PDF or print on a phone

        Microsoft Word app: open the downloaded file, then the ⋯ (More) menu → Save as or Export → PDF; or ⋯ → Print.

        Google Docs: open the file, then ⋮ → Share & export → Save as → PDF document; or ⋮ → Share & export → Print. Google Docs may lay some pages out slightly differently from Word.

        Or use Share / Email or Save to Google Drive above (needs internet and a Google account; the PDF is made by Google Docs and may differ slightly from Word). Privacy

        On a PC the Word file can also be made with python generate_acr.py <exported file> -o ACR.docx; both give the same file.

        Draft in Google Drive

        Your draft is saved in your own Drive folder "ACR Utility - Word and PDF" a few seconds after you stop typing. On another device, open the app and tap "Keep my draft in Google Drive" with the same Google account to continue.

        The copy you don't choose is not deleted: it is kept in your Drive folder as an "other copy" file.
